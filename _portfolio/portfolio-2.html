" collection: portfolio --- This is my copy of Five Nights at Freddy's. Essentially, it is a five minute period, where you have to survive against multiple enemies that will attack you throughout that time period. They all work on random numbers and if statements, in order to move to your office (your base). In your office, you are able to check the cameras, which reveals where all of the enemies are within the building (excluding the kitchen). You can also check if an enemy is in either of your doorways using the lights, and send them back using the doors. If you fail to send them away while they are in your doorway for too long, you lose.
